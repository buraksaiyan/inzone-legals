How attributes work

100 XP adds one rating point, up to 99. Focus sessions, targets, habits, Body Focus, daily wins and journal entries build different attributes.

Overall is the average of all five ratings. Inactive attributes can gradually decay.

Questions

What to know before you start.

Short answers about the free focus timer, accounts, privacy and Premium access.

Is RiseArc free to use?

Yes. You can begin as a guest and run focus sessions up to 45 minutes at a time. That is a per-session limit, not a daily limit. Premium adds longer sessions and additional focus and tracking features.

Is RiseArc a Pomodoro timer?

It includes Focus Blocks with looping 25-minute work and 5-minute break cycles or 50-minute work and 10-minute break cycles. You can also use Flow Focus when you want a straightforward timer without breaks.

Do I need to create an account?

No. RiseArc creates a guest profile after you accept the privacy policy and terms. You can later connect email, Google or Apple sign-in to protect the same progress across devices.

How are the daily grades and stats calculated?

Completed focus sessions, habits, targets, daily wins, Body Focus and journal entries contribute to your day grade and five attributes: Focus, Discipline, Consistency, Physical and Mindfulness. Their average forms your Overall Rating.

Does RiseArc contain ads or sell journal content?

No. RiseArc does not use third-party advertising SDKs, does not show ads and does not use journals or other personal content for advertising or marketing.

Is RiseArc a medical or ADHD treatment app?

No. RiseArc is a productivity tool designed to offer clearer structure and feedback. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent ADHD or any other medical condition.
