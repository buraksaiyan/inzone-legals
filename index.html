Account deletion (inzone)

You can request permanent deletion of your inzone account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1 — Delete via app (instant):

  1. Open inzone
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm to permanently remove your data

Option 2 — Request via email: if the app is uninstalled, email feedback@1inzone.app with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

For your protection, deletion requests must come from the same email address you signed up with. Requests from unmatched or unverified addresses will be rejected.
