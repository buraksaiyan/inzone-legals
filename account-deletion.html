Account controls

Delete your account or data

For inzone: ADHD Focus & Study (com.inzoneapp.inzone)

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open inzone.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm permanent deletion.

This option deletes the signed-in or guest account and its associated inzone data.

Request deletion without the app

Email feedback@1inzone.app with the subject Account Deletion Request. If the account has an email address, send the request from that address. Include the inzone username if available so we can verify the account.

Delete data without deleting the account

Individual journals, habits, targets, and similar entries can be removed inside inzone. Settings also provides a progress reset option. You may email feedback@1inzone.app to request deletion of specific account data.

Data deleted

Account deletion removes the inzone profile and associated app content, including focus-session history, habits, targets, daily wins, journal entries, progress scores, streaks, achievements, profile details, avatar files, and authentication linkage controlled by inzone.

Limited retention

Deletion removes data from active inzone systems after the request is verified and processed. Data may remain temporarily in protected service backups until those backups expire. Minimal security, fraud-prevention, transaction, or legal records may be retained only where required for a legitimate security or legal obligation. Google Play, Apple, and RevenueCat may retain store transaction records under their own policies; inzone does not store full payment-card details.
