### Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: April 4, 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how **Color Hunter** (the "App") handles information when you use our service. We are committed to being transparent about data practices and respecting your privacy.

#### 1. Information We Collect
**We do not collect, store, or process any personal information** such as your name, email address, phone number, or location data. The App does not have user accounts, registration forms, or any mechanism to identify you as an individual.

#### 2. Google AdMob (Third-Party Advertising)
The App uses **Google AdMob**, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display ads and keep the App free. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve personalized advertising:
* Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / IDFA on iOS)
* Device identifiers and technical information (OS version, device model)
* IP address (used for approximate geolocation and fraud prevention)
* Ad interaction and App usage data for analytics

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own Privacy Policy. You can review Google's data practices at: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

#### 3. How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads
You can limit ad personalization at any time through your device settings:
* **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
* **iOS:** Settings → Privacy → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

#### 4. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

#### 5. GDPR & CCPA (Your Rights)
If you are located in the EU/EEA (GDPR) or are a California resident (CCPA), you have specific rights regarding your data (access, deletion, opt-out). Since we do not collect personal data directly, these rights apply to Google AdMob's data collection. You can exercise those rights via [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com) or via Google's Privacy Policy page.

#### 6. Data Security
The App does not transmit any data to our own servers. All ad-related data transmission is handled exclusively by Google AdMob using industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS).

#### 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can reach our developer studio at: **[feedback@1inzone.app](mailto:feedback@1inzone.app)**
